What you will not find here

Two sites stay current — the Vaca Muerta app and the mining title map. Everything else is frozen at the end of 2024. That is enough to judge the method and to see what can be measured without buying the data; it is not enough to run tomorrow's operation on. A fresh series over your own block or district is work, not a download.
