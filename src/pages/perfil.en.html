The work

Where this can be useful

Data and AI for decisions

Dashboards, models and agents on auditable sources or the client's own data: what offset operators are doing, what an asset is worth, where a bid makes sense. The ten live on this site show the full method.

Reservoir engineering

Full-field simulation and geomodelling, reserves progression, development planning, forecasting and waterflood surveillance. Several years as a specialist, working in Petrel, Eclipse and CMG.

Project development

Asset valuation, data rooms, production forecasts and the economics of an acquisition decision. At the other end of the cycle, taking a new project from idea to execution, with the team and the counterparties convened for the case.

The thread across all of it: the incident registry for Argentina's energy sector (fourteen incidents documented row by row) and language-model interpretability research, with a published paper and demo. Both answer the same question: which tasks can already be handed to an AI system, and which cannot yet.

Who with

Track record

The companies, and the work at each

  1. Since 2026

    Engineer

    Podeley · technical solutions for oil, gas and mining · Buenos Aires

    Projects and tools for deciding in energy and mining: dashboards, models and agents on public data and the client's own, and the assembly of projects from idea to execution. Ten of those tools are published and can be opened from this site. Alongside that, research in language-model interpretability: that is where the judgement comes from about when an AI system can carry an operating decision and when it cannot.

  2. 2022–2026

    Energy Transition Engineer, corporate development

    Pluspetrol · Buenos Aires

    Appraisal of new-business projects through to the decision to invest or not, with the timely shelving when the economics did not hold. Most of it was sustainable aviation fuel, with UOP, Technip and LanzaJet, and with the largest bioenergy companies in Argentina and Brazil as counterparties; it reached conceptual engineering and none went to execution. Across the same desk passed bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS), helium and uranium, among other ideas, plus the projects to create gas demand: ammonia and urea for export off both coasts, and gas solutions for frac fleets. What that stage left behind is the judgement, not the technology: in new energy the bottleneck is rarely the process, it is who signs the offtake.

  3. 2019–2022

    Reservoir Engineer, new technologies and global reserves

    Pluspetrol · Buenos Aires

    Global reserves progression and asset appraisals, with the Ecuadorian fields still in the portfolio, the development of the Ichoa play in Bolivia and the Camisea integrated model, on the operator's side. On the new side, underground gas storage.

  4. 2014–2019

    Reservoir Engineer

    Tecpetrol · Buenos Aires, with assets in Peru, Ecuador and Colombia

    Tecpetrol's technical representative in the Camisea consortium, blocks 88 and 56, alongside operator Pluspetrol and partners Hunt Oil, SK, Repsol and Sonatrach. The brief: migration and maintenance of the full-field simulation model of the giant gas condensate field, and lead of the integrated asset modelling study across GAP, PROSPER and MBAL. In Ecuador, mature waterflooded fields for Petroamazonas EP: Shushufindi, in the consortium led by Schlumberger (now SLB), and Libertador-Atacapi, through Pardaliservices, a venture with Schlumberger, Canacol Energy and Sertecpet. In parallel, development models for Pendare in Colombia. From that stage also come the co-creation of Well Expert, the well-data web application the company ended up running on, and the geosciences focal point role in that rollout.

  5. 2008–2014

    Reservoir Engineer

    Pluspetrol · Neuquén basin and corporate

    Reservoir engineering for the El Porvenir area: studies, reserves progression, development plan, forecasts, waterflood surveillance and workover design. At Centenario, surveillance and more than 40 interventions, with field training on rigs, stimulation and production. In corporate engineering, valuation support to business development and the integrated study of block 1AB in the Marañón basin. Out of that desk came the bid for Petroandina Resources, which ended in the purchase of its share of Argentine assets for over US$ 300 million.

  6. 2016–2021, alongside

    Instructor and researcher

    Instituto Tecnológico de Buenos Aires · Petroleum Engineering Department

    Five years teaching Petroleum Project Appraisal, and two as a researcher at the Reservoir Simulation Research Center. Two publications came out of it, on applying reservoir simulation techniques to lithium brine salars: the reason this site carries mining demos.

Before that: research analyst for an investment bank's equity team, and an internship at Baker Oil Tools. The full detail is in the CV.
