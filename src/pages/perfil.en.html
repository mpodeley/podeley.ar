Track record

Where I worked, and on what

  1. Since 2026

    Engineer

    Podeley · technical solutions for oil, gas and mining · Buenos Aires

    Dashboards, models and agents built on public data and on the client's own, for market intelligence, opportunity framing and decision support.   2. 2022–2026

    Energy Transition Engineer, corporate development

    Pluspetrol · Buenos Aires

    Appraising new-business projects through to the decision to invest or not, and shelving them in time when the economics did not hold. Most of it was sustainable aviation fuel, with UOP, Technip, LanzaJet and AFRY; we reached conceptual engineering and none went to execution. What stayed with me is the judgement, not the technology: in new energy the bottleneck is rarely the process, it is who signs the offtake. There were also projects to create gas demand: ammonia and urea for export off both coasts, and gas solutions for frac fleets.

  3. 2019–2022

    Reservoir Engineer, new technologies and global reserves

    Pluspetrol · Buenos Aires

    Global reserves progression and asset appraisals, with the Ecuadorian fields still in the portfolio and the development of the Ichoa play in Bolivia. On the new side, underground gas storage and bioenergy with carbon capture and storage.

  4. 2014–2019

    Reservoir Engineer

    Tecpetrol · Buenos Aires, with assets in Peru, Ecuador and Colombia

    Tecpetrol's technical representative in the Camisea consortium, blocks 88 and 56: I migrated and maintained the full-field simulation model of a giant gas condensate field, and led the integrated asset modelling study across GAP, PROSPER and MBAL. Alongside that, development models for Pendare in Colombia and mature waterflooded fields at Libertador-Atacapi and Shushufindi in Ecuador. I was also co-creator of Well Expert, the well-data web application the company ended up running on, and the geosciences focal point for that rollout.

  5. 2008–2014

    Reservoir Engineer

    Pluspetrol · Neuquén basin and corporate

    Reservoir engineering for the El Porvenir area: studies, reserves progression, development plan, forecasts, waterflood surveillance and workover design. At Centenario, surveillance and more than 40 interventions, with field training on rigs, stimulation and production. In corporate engineering, valuation support to business development and the integrated study of block 1AB in the Marañón basin.

  6. 2016–2021, alongside

    Instructor and researcher

    Instituto Tecnológico de Buenos Aires · Petroleum Engineering Department

    Five years teaching Petroleum Project Appraisal, and two as a researcher at the Reservoir Simulation Research Center. Two publications came out of it, on applying reservoir simulation techniques to lithium brine salars — which is why this site carries mining demos.

Before that: research analyst for an investment bank's equity team, and an internship at Baker Oil Tools. The full detail is in the CV.
