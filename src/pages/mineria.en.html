Know who holds the ground before making a move

Argentina has no national mining cadastre: each province publishes its own in a different format, and several publish nothing at all. This unifies six into one map — 19,671 tenements, 24 million hectares — with the holder, the mineral and the file number behind every polygon. San Juan has 76.7% of its territory under mining title; Jujuy, 70.4%.
