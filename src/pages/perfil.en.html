A fair objection

Building tools is not something I started last year

At Tecpetrol I co-created Well Expert, a web application to manage, visualise and analyse well data, and I was the geosciences focal point for its company-wide rollout. That was between 2014 and 2019, with the tooling of the time. What I do now is the same idea at a different cost.

The same reason explains why a petroleum engineer's site carries mining demos: two of my publications apply reservoir simulation techniques to lithium brine salars, presented at the Salta and Catamarca lithium seminars. A salar models much like a reservoir, and few people look at both sides.
