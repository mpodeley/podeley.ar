AI workflows

The engineer defines and verifies; the agent builds

A project for a reservoir, production, or development team to try AI agents on its own data and decide where to go deeper. It runs on the company's platform and coexists with AI initiatives in other departments. The idea behind it is in the talk "Trabajar como un centauro" (in Spanish).

1

Diagnosis

Interviews with the team and with IT: data, models, spreadsheets and scripts, where they live and who maintains them, and which decisions are made today on incomplete information.

2

Sandbox

An isolated environment for working with agents: read-only copies of the data, a log of every action, git, and open software such as OPM Flow, ResInsight, Python, and DuckDB. The company picks the language model, local or commercial under contract.

3

Proofs of concept

Two or three, each with its success criterion written down before it starts. For example, an agent that assists the history match of a model, automated well surveillance, or a decline and type-well dashboard on the company's own data platform.

4

Workshops

Hands-on half days, open to more people than the users of the proofs: git, change review, and working with agents on what was built.

5

Roadmap

For each topic, what the proof showed, the data available, the effort, and who sustains it. What to pursue further, which training comes next, and what is worth institutionalizing.

The proofs are prototypes: an assisted match falls short of a history match with a forecast, and their forecasts cannot be used to certify reserves. Technical judgment stays with the team; code and data stay with the company.
