Two published tools

System status and transport network

Daily demand, linepack, and injection, plus monthly series for 76 transport segments. Bolivian supply fell from 12.7 MMm³/d in July 2022 to 0.3 in 2025, and unconventional gas filled the difference. Eight segments exceed 100% because the denominator is nameplate capacity.

ENARGAS and CAMMESA · system current daily · network monthly, data to 2025-09

Bid-price simulator for power auctions

Merit-order dispatch and Monte Carlo over hydrology, demand, gas price, and renewable resources in Peru's SEIN. It calculates the outcome distribution for each bid price. The model runs in the browser and the dispatch engine is unit-tested.
