The mining rights of seven provinces, unified

Seven provincial cadastres unified into one map: 21,386 tenements, 25.8 million hectares and 2,991 holders, with the mineral and the file number behind every polygon. San Juan has 66.4% of its territory under mining title. Where information is missing, the map shows it as explicit absence, never as open ground.

Rank drill targets before paying for the field campaign

The block holds 1,877 km² of usable ground, and screening cuts it to 15 targets with coordinates. It is settled blind: the method re-finds Pirquitas and Chinchillas within 20 and 80 meters of the alteration footprint already mapped. Delivered as shapefile and GeoJSON, for your GIS.

Audit a mine's excavated volume, from the outside

Differencing elevation models from different dates, Veladero gives 424 Mm³ excavated and 497 Mm³ deposited between 2000 and 2024, checkable against its reports. At Haerwusu, China's largest open-pit coal mine, the 1,742 Mm³ is an independent measurement where no production gets published.

Each salar's buildout, dated year by year

Atacama holds 30.3 km² of ponds against 9.0 at Hombre Muerto, and built area correlates 0.81 with declared exports once you lag it two years, which is how long brine takes to work through the pond chain. An announced expansion without ponds under construction gives itself away. Area is not production: direct extraction produces with far fewer ponds.
