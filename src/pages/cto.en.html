Scope

What the role covers

Technical leadership connects operating work with technology decisions. It covers subsurface tools, production and commercial data, automation, and AI.

The engagement runs month to month. Priorities, contracts, and the split of work between people and models are reviewed on an agreed schedule.

For assignments with a defined end: consults and projects.

When it fits

Part-time leadership

This format is for a small or midsize operator, a gas marketer, a junior miner, or a new company that needs technical direction without filling a full-time position.

It does not include IT support, networks, or ERP administration. Work that requires a larger build gets its own internal or external project team.
