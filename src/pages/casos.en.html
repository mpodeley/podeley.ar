Exploration and production 4 pieces

What the basin is doing before the official record says so. All four, with scope and caveats: E&P.

  • Decline curves, EUR and type wells

    Arps fits and type wells by block, to set your own well beside the one the offset operator drilled at the same lateral length and the same spacing.

    Chapter IV, SecEnergía · Neuquén basin · current
  • Activity from night lights

    Covers the 13.5 months Chapter IV runs late: confirms 71% of 20,178 well events and estimates the rest, at ROC-AUC 0.85 for drilling and 0.91 for fracturing.

    VIIRS + Chapter IV · Vaca Muerta · 2019–2026
  • Ground deformation from InSAR

    Seven years of series over the basin. The injecting concession and the producing one next door move in opposite directions, 80 mm each, and the edge between them is what shears a casing.

    Sentinel-1 track 18 · 210 × 210 km · 2019–2026
  • Emissions by operator

    113 flares and 2.4 Mt of CO₂ in 2024, attributed by concession: between 2.1 and 31.9 kg of CO₂ per barrel of oil equivalent depending on who operates.

    VIIRS Nightfire + Chapter IV · Neuquén basin · 2024
Mining 4 pieces

What sits over the ground, and what gets measured without walking it. All four, with scope and caveats: mining.

  • Unified mining claim registry

    21,386 claims, 25.8 million hectares and 2,991 holders across seven provinces on one map, with the mineral and the case file behind every polygon.

    Provincial registries · data to 2026-08 · open dataset
  • Exploration targets from spectrometry

    1,877 km² of usable ground narrowed to 15 targets with coordinates. Tested blind: it re-finds Pirquitas and Chinchillas within 20 and 80 meters of the mapped footprint.

    ASTER + EMIT · Puna de Jujuy · shapefile and GeoJSON
  • Excavated volume from elevation model differencing

    Veladero comes out at 424 Mm³ excavated and 497 deposited between 2000 and 2024. At Haerwusu, where no production is published, the 1,742 Mm³ is the only independent measurement.

    SRTM 2000 + ASTER stereo · 2000–2024
  • Lithium brine pond buildout, year by year

    Five salars measured back to 1985. Built pond surface leads declared exports by two years at a correlation of 0.81, so an expansion announced without construction shows up.

    Landsat · Atacama and 4 Puna salars · 1985–2025

Which material each sensor can separate, band by band: the 3D globe of the fleet.

Cybersecurity in energy 3 pieces

What the sector exposes, with no one to report it to. The three pieces and the reasoning: cyber.

  • Cyber incident registry

    Fourteen cases in Argentine energy and critical infrastructure between 2020 and 2026, with a source on every row and profiles of the eleven attacking groups. Seven surfaced through neither the company nor a regulator.

    Open registry · downloadable dataset · CC BY 4.0
  • Attack surface map

    Six layers built on open sources: network structure, cascading impact, incidents, per-operator network exposure, satellite activity and governance. It publishes the aggregate, never the target.

    Open sources · nothing scanned · ES and EN
  • Memo for a sector CERT

    What the first year of the US oil & gas ISAC cost, how the Danish SektorCERT works, and three budget scenarios for building the Argentine equivalent.

    Costs from tax filings · two draft regulations
