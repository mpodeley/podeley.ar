Engagements

Consult, project, or ongoing support

A consult resolves one decision. A project delivers a defined result. Ongoing support covers decisions that keep coming back.

Days

One-off consult

A second opinion on a vendor proposal, a data room review, or a technical assumption before signing. Most consults take one or two meetings.

Weeks

Project

Scope, timeline, and deliverables set in writing. The result may be a diagnosis, a model, a dashboard, or a full build, with a handover at the end.

Ongoing

Standing engagement

Technical direction for periodic decisions about what to build, buy, automate, and staff. The full scope is on the CTO page.

Process

From inquiry to handover

1

First conversation

Twenty minutes to define the decision, the deadline, and the information available.

2

Scope and proposal

Scope options, timeline, cost, and deliverables in writing. When useful, the proposal includes a mockup.

3

Execution and handover

The result is documented and, when it includes a tool, deployed to the agreed environment. The team that will use it receives a handover.

If the problem falls outside this scope, that gets settled in the first conversation.
