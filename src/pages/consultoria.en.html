The process

Four steps

The work is with business development, subsurface, marketing and strategy teams: one concrete decision, incomplete information, a deadline.

1

The call

Twenty minutes at no cost: what problems you have coming and which ones this work can help with. The tools on this site are the inspiration: that kind of thing, with your operation's own data inside.

2

The proposal

Within that same week, and at no cost when the fit is there: scope, timeline and deliverables in writing. When the case calls for it, it ships with a mockup or demo of the solution, so the discussion runs on something concrete instead of a document.

3

The scope

Before anything starts we talk scope alternatives and roadmap. More depth, more iterations or extra specialists cost more; if a simple, approximate solution is enough to decide, it costs you less. That trade-off is yours to pick, with the options on the table.

4

The delivery

Built, maintained and handed over: the tool ends up running wherever you decide, with your team trained, or an outsourced one if you don't have it yet. No permanent dependency on the consultant.

If the call shows there is no way to help, that gets said on the spot: a proposal only arrives when there is something worth building.

What you get

A deliverable that stays running

Whatever the decision calls for: a diagnosis with sources and assumptions in the open, a model your team runs on its own, or a dashboard like the ones on this site with your data inside. The proposal pins it down in writing: what gets delivered, when, and what stays on your side at the end.

What it takes

Three things from your side

The decision

What you need to resolve and by when. The more concrete it is, the better the proposal comes out.

Whatever data you have

Someone on your side who knows the asset. Nobody knows it like the people operating it.
