See competitor activity 13.5 months before the official registry does

With the registry running 13.5 months behind, the tail of the series looks like an activity collapse that never happened. Night-lights cover that blind window: they confirm 71% of 20,178 known well events and estimate the missing ones at ROC-AUC 0.85 for drilling and 0.91 for fracking. At 500 m resolution the unit is the pad cluster, not the well.

Watch ground deformation without field instruments

What shears a casing is not subsidence but the difference in subsidence between two nearby points, and the map locates those edges. Two adjacent leases move as mirror images for seven years: the one injecting rises 80 mm, the one producing sinks 80 mm. It measures movement, not cause.

Know where you land on emissions intensity before someone else publishes it

Banks and funds ask for emissions intensity, and that number shouldn't depend on the counterparty declaring it. 113 flares detected and 2.4 Mt of CO₂ in 2024, attributed by lease and divided by declared production: between 2.1 and 31.9 kg of CO₂ per barrel of oil equivalent depending on the operator. Methane is measured basin-wide; attributing it by company isn't possible yet.
