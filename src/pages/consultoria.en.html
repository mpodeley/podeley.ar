The process

Four steps, the first two free

1

The call

Twenty minutes, free: what problems you have coming and which ones I can help with. The tools on this site are the inspiration — that kind of thing gets built from public data, and yours can fold in your operation's own.

2

The proposal

Within that same week, and if I see a fit, also free: scope, timeline and deliverables in writing. When the case calls for it, it ships with a mockup or demo of the solution, so we argue about something concrete instead of a document.

3

The scope

Before anything starts we talk scope alternatives and roadmap. More depth, more iterations or extra specialists cost more; if a simple, approximate solution is enough to decide, it costs you less. That trade-off is yours to pick, with the options on the table.

4

The delivery

Built, maintained and handed over: the tool ends up running wherever you say, with your team trained — or an outsourced one if you don't have it yet. No permanent dependency on me.

If the call shows I can't help you, I say so right there: a proposal only arrives when there is something worth building.
